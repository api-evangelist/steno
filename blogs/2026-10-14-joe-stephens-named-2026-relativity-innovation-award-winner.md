Relativity has named Steno’s Joe Stephens, JD, the 2026 Best Innovator for Legal Education. The award recognizes professors at academic institutions who are advancing the technical competency of the next generation of legal professionals. Joe's win was announced on October 1 during Relativity Fest in Chicago.
