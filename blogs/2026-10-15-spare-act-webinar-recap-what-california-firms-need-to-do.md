Steno’s Jeff Karotkin, Senior Director of Court Filing and Service of Process, spent a recent webinar walking attendees through what changes. Jeff was a stakeholder throughout the bill’s drafting, representing the California Association of Legal Support Professionals . Here’s what he covered.
